# Changelog

Pipsy version **1.0.0**. Newest changes first.

## 2026-11-16

* Section library: general sections stay available on every page; Pipsy home sections show only on the home page, and content page sections only on page templates. Removed unused sections: Hero slider, Image accordion, Scene tabs, Lookbook, Collection index, Custom section, Product questions, Product story and Size finder.
* Block library: product blocks appear only in product sections; Image banner, Popup, Group, tabs and accordion rows list the general blocks. Removed unused blocks: Video button, and the block versions of Journal articles and Featured collection (the sections stay). The second Heading entry is gone from the picker.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-15

First public release.

* Theme info in **Customize > Theme settings** links to this guide and to Novaworks support.

* Quick view: even 50/50 columns on every page, inner padding on the home page, tighter buy box and compact pickers.
* Demo links use `shopify://` references and hide themselves when the collection, page or blog does not exist on the store ([How links behave](links.md)).
* Related products leave no empty gap when there is nothing to recommend.
* Product reviews: new Reviews band with a summary card and a reviews app block (tested with Judge.me), stars on cards and by the product title, and an editor-only preview score.
* Cart page: order note and shipping estimate as two side-by-side cards with a Save note button.
* One shared overlay for quick view, cart drawer and search drawer.
* Logo: SVG logo files for the demo; text wordmark when no logo is uploaded; footer logo setting.

## 2026-11-14

* Footer rebuilt: sign-up card, promise row, larger wordmark, social chips, ruled legal row with back to top.

## 2026-11-12

* New merchant settings: heading size scale (desktop and mobile), back to top button, badge tag prefix, Share buttons product block, product media position, search drawer recommended products, cart drawer accelerated checkout, popup "home page only" and "hide for signed-in customers", marquee pause on hover, footer Follow on Shop.
* Main menu with four top-level items (Shop, Collections, Pages, Contact) and one Shop mega panel.
* One spacing rhythm between home sections.
* Gift card page and gift card product template.
* Collections list, plain page and policy pages restyled; customer account pages restyled.

## 2026-11-11

* Search results page, cart page, catalog page and product page rebuilt.
* Home page mobile pass; Pipsy-styled size selects; search drawer matched to the bag drawer.

## 2026-11-10

* Bag drawer redesigned (520 px, Pipsy footer with promise line).
* Product cards: the add action is a text pill under the photo.
* Content pages: About, Contact, FAQs, Order tracking, Size guide, Journal list and article, 404 and password page.
* Header, mega menu, mobile drawer and footer; home page sections with scroll motion, hero product pins, colour dots on the rack, SVG review stars.
* Colour swatches on cards and in the swatch configuration.
* Demo data generated and imported on the demo store.

## 2026-11-09

* First version: Pipsy tokens, typography (Bagel Fat One headings, Figtree body) and the Pipsy line icon set.
